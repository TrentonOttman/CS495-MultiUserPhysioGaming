## Sponsor Planning Notes

- Project contains multiple repositories.
- Scope is focused around implementing multiplayer and game design.
- Wants to support 8 - 12 streams of data in real time.
- Should have modular input sensitivity.
- Games should be fun over educational. Educational games are subject to too much scrutiny.
- One continuous signal per player e.g. at some threshold, jump.
- Success would look like a group of our classmates playing the game during our final presentation. 
- Brain ball & Playroom can be used as inspiration.
- Multiplayer implementation is the highest priority. There is currently none, not even prototypes.
- Must be a web app.
