# Multiplayer Decision

- We have decided on Colyseus to implement our Multiplayer
- It is server authoritative, so game balancing will be more feasible at the cost of being more work
  - Balance in terms of better syncing and preventing cheating
  - Better support relative to being on lower-end laptops
- Colyseus is free and scalable for our purposes
- Best option left after nixing Unity's path
  - If we went with Unity, he would've strongly preferred using Unity's built in multiplayer support
- Sponsor didn't love Unity, and mentioned preferring server-authoritative if at all possible
Thus, our final decision is Colyseus
